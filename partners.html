Who can partner with us in India?

What is a medical tourism facilitator?
A medical tourism facilitator connects patients with hospitals abroad and coordinates everything around treatment: sharing reports with consent, specialist consultations, visa letters, travel, accommodation, and support on the ground. The Strategic Traveller is a facilitator for patients from New Zealand travelling to accredited hospitals in India.
Last updated: 2 October 2026

How it works

How does a partnership work?

01
Send an enquiry
Tell us about your organisation, location, and what you offer.
02
Verification
We check accreditation and licences, including ART Act registration for fertility clinics and transplant authorisation where relevant, plus reviews and a site visit or video tour.
03
Agreement
We agree referral terms, rates, data-sharing and patient-consent rules, and points of contact in writing. Commissions are disclosed to patients.
04
Referrals and review
We start referring patients and review feedback together regularly.

We only partner with accredited hospitals and licensed providers. Patient wellbeing comes first in every recommendation, so referrals are always based on what is right for the patient.

Frequently asked

How can a hospital in India get international patients from New Zealand?

Work with a facilitator that has a presence in New Zealand. Our New Zealand-based lead meets patients, gathers their reports, and prepares them for travel, and our Delhi team coordinates with your international patient department. Send us an enquiry to start the verification process.

What does a medical tourism facilitator do for hospitals?

A facilitator finds and prepares international patients, shares case summaries, schedules tele-consultations, handles visa letters, travel, and accommodation, and supports the patient on the ground. We can also help hospitals develop their international patient programme and marketing for New Zealand.

Can hotels partner with a medical tourism agency?

Yes. We work with hotels and serviced apartments near partner hospitals for patient and companion stays, usually of one to four weeks. We look for accessible rooms, clean and quiet surroundings, suitable meals, and staff comfortable supporting patients in recovery.

Which cities are you looking for partners in?

Mainly Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, and Mumbai for hospitals, hotels, and transport, and Kerala and Rishikesh for recovery and wellness centres. In New Zealand, we work with partners across the country.

How are referral terms agreed?

We agree terms in writing before any referral, covering rates, responsibilities, and points of contact. Terms are disclosed transparently, and the patient's interest always comes first.
